---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do rewards come from?">
    According to the platform's business model, rewards are allocated from partner platform service fees to eligible members who complete valid tasks under current platform rules.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are account funds used as rewards?">
    No. Account funds are used for task participation or fulfillment guarantee, not as the reward source.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does win or loss affect commission?">
    No. Commission is based on eligible task completion and system settlement, not match outcomes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is an eligible task?">
    An eligible task is a task displayed by the platform, completed according to its stated requirements, and confirmed as valid by system records.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between an Active Member and a Valid Member?">
    An active member is a member who has accumulated deposits of at least 50 USDT. A valid member is a member who has accumulated deposits of at least 100 USDT.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Personal Commission and Team Commission?">
    Personal Commission is earned from your own eligible tasks. Team Commission is earned from your downline members’ eligible commissions and is calculated according to the tier rates associated with your rank.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Team Commission calculated from downline deposits?">
    No. Team Commission is calculated from eligible commission earnings generated by members in Layers 1–3, multiplied by your applicable layer rate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Agent Rank based only on account balance?">
    No. Agent Rank may also require Valid Layer 1 Members and Valid Members in Layers 2–3. All applicable conditions must be met.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
