title: "Where are the rewards come from? "
sidebarTitle: "Reward Sources"
description: "NMB Match rewards come from partner platform service fees, not from user deposits or game outcomes, and are earned by completing valid tasks."
---

Rewards on NMB Match are generated through a simple model where partner platforms pay for player activity and those service fees are shared with users who complete tasks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Partner Platforms Need Players">
    Many new or smaller CSGO platforms lack real players and active matches, so they struggle to grow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We Provide Real Match Activity">
    We connect real users to complete real match tasks, helping platforms increase match volume and activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Platforms Pay Service Fees">
    Partner platforms pay us service fees for valid traffic, real matches, and improved platform activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Users Receive Task Rewards">
    We share part of the service revenue with users who complete the tasks as their rewards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key Points

### Not from user deposits

Rewards do not come from user deposits or account balances.

### Not based on win or loss

Match results do not affect task rewards. Win or lose, tasks completed = rewards.

### From partner platform service fees

Rewards come from service fees paid by partner platforms, not from game outcomes or user funds.

<Note>
  **Platforms buy activity → Users complete tasks → Rewards come from service fees.**
</Note>
