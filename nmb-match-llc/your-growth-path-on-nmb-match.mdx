To build your presence on NMB Match, follow the platform’s steps for participation, task completion, commission earnings, team building, and rank progression.

## Steps to participate at NMB MATCH

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join NMB Match">
    Learn the business model and platform rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Eligible Tasks">
    Select and complete platform tasks according to the stated requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Personal Commission">
    After task validation and settlement, Personal Commission is calculated according to your Agent Rank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a Real 3-Layer Team">
    Invite and support real members across L1, L2, and L3.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unlock Agent Rank Benefits">
    Meet the requirements and gradually access more platform benefits.
  </Step>
</Steps>
