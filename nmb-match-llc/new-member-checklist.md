## Understand the Platform

Before you begin, take a few minutes to understand what **NMB Match** is and how the platform works.

NMB Match is a platform where users can access available activities or tasks, follow the required instructions, and track eligible earnings or rewards through their accounts.

### What you should know

* **Account:** Your personal profile used to access NMB Match.
* **Tasks:** Activities available for eligible users to complete.
* **USDT:** A digital asset that may be used for balances, rewards, deposits, or withdrawals, depending on how NMB Match is configured.
* **Earnings:** The amount credited to your account after eligible activities are successfully completed and approved.
* **Invitations:** A feature that allow you to invite other people to join NMB Match.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Always review the platform's official terms, task requirements, fees, reward conditions, and withdrawal rules before committing funds or completing activities.
{% endhint %}

## Secure Your Account

Account security should be completed before you start using financial or task-related features.

### Use a strong password

Create a password that is unique to NMB Match. Avoid reusing a password from your email, social media, banking, or cryptocurrency accounts.

A strong password should ideally include:

* Uppercase and lowercase letters
* Numbers
* Special characters
* Sufficient length

### Enable additional security

NMB Match supports these features, enable them:

* **Two-factor authentication (2FA)**
* **Email verification**
* **Withdrawal/security PIN**

### Protect yourself

* Never share your password, verification code, recovery phrase, private key, or 2FA code.
* Do not allow another person to remotely access your device.
* Verify the website address before signing in.
* Be cautious of unofficial support accounts and unsolicited messages.

{% hint style="warning" %}
NMB Match administrators or support staff should never need your password, private key, or wallet recovery phrase.
{% endhint %}

## Learn About USDT

**USDT (Tether)** is a digital token commonly designed to track the value of the U.S. dollar. NMB Match may use USDT for certain platform transactions, balances, rewards, or withdrawals.

### Key terms

**Wallet**\
A cryptocurrency wallet is used to send, receive, and store supported digital assets.

**Wallet address**\
A unique address used to receive cryptocurrency.

**Network**\
USDT exists on multiple blockchain networks. Examples may include TRON (TRC20), Ethereum (ERC20), and others.

**Transaction fee**\
A fee may apply when transferring USDT. The amount and fee depend on the platform and blockchain network being used.

{% hint style="danger" %}
