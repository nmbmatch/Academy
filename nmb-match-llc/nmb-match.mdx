NMB Match is a competitive gaming platform connecting real players, partner gaming platforms, and esports ecosystem resources.

## Real Player Participation

Helping partner platforms increase real activity through real tasks and matchmaking participation.

## Esports Ecosystem Connection

Connecting platforms, players, esports teams, streamers, and content creators.

## Matchmaking Liquidity Services

Providing partner gaming platforms with player activity, match volume, and matchmaking efficiency support.

## Member Growth Mechanism

Members gradually explore platform benefits through task participation, team growth, and Agent Rank.

We support partner platforms through real task participation, matchmaking activity, and player liquidity services.

Members can participate through eligible tasks and gradually explore Personal Commission, team growth, and Agent Rank benefits.

<Warning>
  **Important:** All rewards and commissions are subject to platform rules, system records, audits, and final settlement.
</Warning>
