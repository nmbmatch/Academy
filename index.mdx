Since its establishment in 2000, our parent group has remained committed to its original vision of "providing high-quality and enjoyable games for players around the world." With more than 25 years of experience in the gaming industry, the group has developed extensive expertise in game operations, player engagement, digital entertainment, mobile gaming, and global gaming ecosystems. This long-term industry foundation provides NMB Match with valuable operational knowledge, technology experience, market insight, and a deep understanding of player behavior and gaming platform development. NMB Match's competitive gaming and esports ecosystem businesses are operated by NMB Match LLC, a U.S.-based operating company responsible for platform operations, technology services, business development, ecosystem partnerships, and global market expansion. Supported by the parent group's gaming expertise and industry experience, our U.S. operating team has a strong understanding of competitive gaming platforms, matchmaking operations, player liquidity, virtual game item markets, esports content, and ecosystem development.
