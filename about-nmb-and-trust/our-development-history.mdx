A timeline of key milestones from 2005 through planned global expansion in 2028.

## 2005–2009

### 2005

- Launched early mobile content services.
- Entered mobile publishing and integrated development resources.

### 2006

- Core online service reached a major concurrency milestone.
- Infrastructure was upgraded for high-traffic live operations.

### 2007

- A casual/adventure title gained industry recognition.
- Publishing operations became more mature and repeatable.

### 2008

- Specialized service quality received repeated recognition.
- A flagship online product maintained category leadership.

### 2009

- Partnered with a major sports / entertainment event.
- Connected online community programs with offline exposure.

## 2010–2013

### 2010

- Integrated multiple development and publishing teams.
- External acquisition improved the overall business structure.

### 2011

- Invested in a mobile-focused team for smart-device growth.
- Reorganized content, media, and platform businesses.

### 2012

- Established a dedicated mobile business unit.
- Created regional branches for localized operations.

### 2013

- Added a new operating branch for local service support.
- Several mobile products reached top revenue positions.

## 2014–2017

### 2014

- Brand value received external recognition.
- Major capital was introduced and downloads passed a milestone.

### 2015

- Formed a strategic alliance with an external partner.
- Acquired an overseas studio and strengthened publishing capacity.

### 2016

- A flagship product achieved strong market performance.
- Multiple titles reached leading revenue positions.

### 2017

- Overseas studio acquisition expanded the global network.
- A capital-market milestone opened a new development stage.

## 2018–2021

### 2018

- Established a public-interest foundation and an intelligent R&D center.
- Improved work systems and management structure.

### 2019

- Mobile publishing capability remained industry-leading.
- Education and accessibility programs continued to expand.

### 2020

- A cross-category acquisition expanded the ecosystem.
- New mobile and casual digital products launched in external markets.

### 2021

- A new integrated office campus began operation.
- Overseas content companies and teams were acquired.

## 2022–2025

### 2022

- Partnerships and service launches expanded the digital ecosystem.
- The first sustainability report was released.

### 2023

- Virtual-content initiatives expanded into new formats.
- Sustainability and public-interest efforts continued.

### 2024

- Leadership service responsibilities were adjusted.
- New multi-platform products launched across several markets.

### 2025

- A major online digital-culture exhibition space opened.
- Cross-platform reach grew.

## 2026–2028

### 2026

- **February:** U.S. operations established in Texas, laying the foundation for global expansion.

### 2027 · Upcoming

- **March:** Planned establishment of the Australia entity to expand into Oceania.
- **September:** Planned establishment of the Hong Kong entity to strengthen the Greater China market.

### 2028 · Upcoming

- **May:** Planned establishment of the UK entity to expand into Europe.
