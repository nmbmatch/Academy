Learn how to find available tasks, understand requirements, and track eligible rewards or earnings inside NMB MATCH.

<Info>
  **Note:** Task availability, requirements, rewards, and eligibility may change. Participation does not guarantee a specific level of income.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign In to NMB MATCH">
    Open your account and go to the **Tasks**, **Missions**, or equivalent section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Available Tasks">
    Open a task and read the full requirements before starting.

    Check:

    - Task instructions
    - Eligibility requirements
    - Completion conditions
    - Reward amount or benefit
    - Time limit, if any
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the Task">
    Tap or click the action shown on screen to begin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Requirements">
    Follow the instructions exactly. Some tasks may require multiple steps before they are marked complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit or Confirm Completion">
    If the task requires manual submission, upload or submit the required information through the official NMB MATCH interface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Your Status">
    Check the task history or rewards section to see whether the task is:

    - In progress
    - Under review
    - Completed
    - Rewarded
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practice

<Warning>
  Keep a record of important task confirmations and never send funds or credentials to individuals claiming they can complete tasks on your behalf.
</Warning>
