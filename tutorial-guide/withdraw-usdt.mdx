**Screenshot examples:** Use your own account and payment details. Copy wallet addresses from your current account, never from an image. Fees, limits, and available options may differ.

This tutorial shows a transfer **from NMB MATCH to Binance**. Prepare your [transaction PIN](/tutorials/set-transaction-pin) and access to the contact method used for verification.

**Check the destination:** Copy the USDT deposit address from your own Binance account and select the same supported network on both platforms. This example uses **BSC (BEP20)**. Review current limits and fees before submitting.

## Step 1: Open your NMB MATCH wallet

Tap the **wallet icon**.

![NMB MATCH navigation with the wallet icon highlighted for withdrawal.](/images/nmb-match/withdraw-01.jpg)

## Step 2: Open withdrawal settings

1. Tap **Withdraw**.
2. Select **Crypto** under **Category**, as shown in the withdrawal form.
3. Select **USDT**.
4. Select **USDT (BEP20)** for this example.
5. Tap **Add Withdrawal Address**.

![NMB MATCH withdrawal screen showing the Crypto category, USDT, BEP20, and Add Withdrawal Address.](/images/nmb-match/withdraw-02.jpg)

## Step 3: Prepare to add your receiving address

In the address form, select **USDT** and **USDT (BEP20)**. Leave the form open while you obtain your Binance deposit address in Steps 4-8.

![NMB MATCH withdrawal address form with USDT and BEP20 selected.](/images/nmb-match/withdraw-03.jpg)

## Step 4: Open Binance Add Funds

Open Binance and tap **Add Funds**.

![Binance home screen with Add Funds highlighted for a deposit.](/images/nmb-match/withdraw-04.jpg)

## Step 5: Open crypto deposit

Tap **Deposit Asset**, as labeled in the supplied screenshot. In other versions of Binance, the equivalent option may be called **On-Chain Deposit**.

![Binance funding options with Deposit Asset highlighted.](/images/nmb-match/withdraw-05.jpg)

## Step 6: Select USDT

Choose **USDT** from the asset list.

![Binance deposit asset selector with USDT highlighted.](/images/nmb-match/withdraw-06.jpg)

## Step 7: Select the matching network

Select **BSC (BEP20)**, matching the network selected in NMB MATCH.

![Binance deposit network selector with BSC BEP20 highlighted.](/images/nmb-match/withdraw-07.jpg)

## Step 8: Copy your Binance deposit address

Tap the copy icon beside your deposit address. Use the current address displayed in your Binance account.

![Binance USDT deposit screen showing the wallet address copy control.](/images/nmb-match/withdraw-08.jpg)

## Step 9: Add the address in NMB MATCH

1. Return to the **Add Withdrawal Address** form opened in Step 3.
2. Paste the Binance deposit address.
3. Check that the pasted address and selected network are correct.
4. Tap **Send Code**.

![NMB MATCH withdrawal address form with the receiving address and Send Code highlighted.](/images/nmb-match/withdraw-09.jpg)

## Step 10: Verify the new address

1. Enter the verification code sent to your email.
2. Complete the on-screen verification if prompted.
3. Tap **Submit** to save the address.

![Withdrawal address verification screen with the code field and Submit highlighted.](/images/nmb-match/withdraw-10.jpg)

## Step 11: Enter the withdrawal amount

Return to the withdrawal form and confirm that your saved Binance address is selected. Enter the amount you want to withdraw, review any displayed fees and limits, then tap **Submit**.

![NMB MATCH withdrawal form with the amount field and Submit highlighted.](/images/nmb-match/withdraw-11.jpg)

## Step 12: Request withdrawal verification

Tap **Send Code** on the withdrawal verification screen.

![NMB MATCH withdrawal verification screen with Send Code highlighted.](/images/nmb-match/withdraw-12.jpg)

## Step 13: Submit the withdrawal request

1. Enter the verification code sent to your email.
2. Enter your **transaction PIN** in the field labeled **Transaction Password** in the supplied screen.
3. Tap **Submit**.
4. Check for confirmation that the withdrawal request was submitted.

Submission is not the same as receipt. Check the withdrawal status in NMB MATCH and your Binance deposit history to confirm the funds arrive. The supplied guide does not specify a processing time.

![NMB MATCH final withdrawal verification showing the code, transaction PIN, and Submit button.](/images/nmb-match/withdraw-13.jpg)
