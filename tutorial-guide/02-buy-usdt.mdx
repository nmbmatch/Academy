USDT is a digital asset commonly used for deposits and withdrawals on crypto-enabled platforms.

<Warning>
  **Important:** Use a reputable, legally available exchange or payment provider in your country. Fees, limits, and verification requirements vary by provider.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a Supported Provider">
    Use a trusted exchange or on-ramp service that supports USDT in your region.

    Examples may include major regulated crypto exchanges or approved local providers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or Sign In to Your Exchange Account">
    Complete any identity verification required by the provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Funds">
    Depending on the provider, you may be able to use:

    - Bank transfer
    - Debit or credit card
    - Supported local payment method
    - Existing crypto balance
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy USDT">
    Search for **USDT**, enter the amount you want to purchase, review the total cost and fees, then confirm the transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Your USDT Balance">
    Confirm that the purchased USDT appears in your exchange or wallet balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Before Depositing to NMB MATCH

<Warning>
  Make sure you know which blockchain network NMB MATCH currently supports for USDT deposits. Sending USDT on the wrong network can result in loss of funds.
</Warning>
