Convert USDT into your local currency through a supported and legally available exchange or provider.

<Warning>
  **Important:** Available cash-out methods, taxes, fees, identity verification, and banking rules depend on your country and provider.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send USDT to a Supported Exchange">
    If your USDT is held in NMB MATCH, withdraw it to a reputable exchange or provider that supports fiat withdrawals in your region.

    Follow the steps in [How to Withdraw USDT](/tutorial-guide/06-withdraw-usdt).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the USDT Deposit">
    Wait until the exchange shows your USDT balance as available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sell or Convert USDT">
    Use the provider's **Sell**, **Convert**, or trading function to exchange USDT into your chosen fiat currency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Exchange Rate and Fees">
    Before confirming, check:

    - Exchange rate
    - Trading or conversion fee
    - Withdrawal fee
    - Final amount you will receive
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw Fiat">
    Choose an available withdrawal method, such as a bank account or another supported local payment method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Receipt">
    Check that the fiat funds have arrived before closing the transaction record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Safety Tips

<Warning>
  Never use unofficial agents who ask you to send USDT directly to a personal wallet in exchange for cash. Prefer regulated or trusted platforms with clear transaction records.
</Warning>
