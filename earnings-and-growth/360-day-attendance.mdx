## Requirements table

| Requirement | Condition |
| --- | :-: |
| Program heading | “360-DAY” and “360 CONSECUTIVE DAY” |
| Daily progress | Complete at least one assigned NMB MATCH daily task for a day to count as valid progress. |
| Missed-day allowance | Up to 15 missed days are permitted during the full program period. |
| Rank at claim | Reach the claimed rank and complete daily tasks under it for at least 60 days. |
| Bonus selection | The bonus depends on rank at the time of claim and completion of all applicable requirements. |

## The 60-day rank requirement

You must reach the rank being claimed and complete daily tasks **under that rank for at least 60 days**.
