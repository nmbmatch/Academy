NMB Match offers reward programs across personal participation, referrals, rank, leadership, and team management.

## Personal Participation

- First Deposit Reward
- Personal Commission

## Referral Bonus

- Direct Referral First Deposit Reward
- Direct Referral Milestone Reward
- Weekly Growth Challenge

## Rank & Leadership

- Agent Rank Reward
- Outstanding Leadership Reward

## Team Management

- Team Activity Reward
- Monthly Leadership Bonus
- Agent Performance Rewards
- 3-Month Agent Performance Reward
- 6-Month Agent Performance Reward
- 9-Month Agent Performance Reward
- 12-Month Agent Performance Reward

<Info>
  Each program has its own active period, eligibility criteria, claim rules, and settlement conditions. Programs may be adjusted or discontinued according to current platform rules.
</Info>
