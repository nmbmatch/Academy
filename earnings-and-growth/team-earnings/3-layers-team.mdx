Your NMB Match team is organized into three layers of downline members, and each layer contributes to your team earnings.

## Layer 1 — Direct Members

Members directly invited by you.

## Layer 2 — Second-Level Members

Members invited by your Layer 1 members.

## Layer 3 — Third-Level Members

Members invited by your Layer 2 members.

Team earnings are calculated based on valid activity within Layers 1–3, your Agent Rank, and platform rules.

Higher Agent Rank may unlock higher team earning rates and greater growth potential.

<Info>
  **Note:** The calculations shown in the source example are based on the Legend Agent Rank.
</Info>
