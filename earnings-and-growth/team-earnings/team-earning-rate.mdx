Team earning rates on NMB Match increase with your Agent Rank across all three layers.

| Agent Rank | Layer 1 — Direct Members | Layer 2 — Second-Level Members | Layer 3 — Third-Level Members |
| --- | --: | --: | --: |
| ROOKIE | 3% | 0% | 0% |
| OPERATOR | 12% | 4% | 2% |
| VETERAN | 14% | 5% | 3% |
| ELITE | 16% | 6% | 4% |
| MASTER | 18% | 7% | 5% |
| LEGEND | 20% | 8% | 6% |

<Info>
  **Higher Agent Rank. Stronger Team Earnings.**
