Team earning rates on NMB Match increase with your Agent Rank across all three layers.

| Agent Rank | Layer 1 | Layer 2 | Layer 3 |
| --- | --: | --: | --: |
| ROOKIE | 16% | 8% | 4% |
| OPERATOR | 16% | 8% | 4% |
| VETERAN | 16% | 8% | 4% |
| ELITE | 16% | 8% | 4% |
| MASTER | 17% | 8% | 4% |
| LEGEND | 18% | 8% | 4% |

<Info>
  **Higher Agent Rank. Higher Team Earnings.**
</Info>
