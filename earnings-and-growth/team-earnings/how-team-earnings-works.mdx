Team earnings on NMB Match are paid to you when your downline members complete eligible tasks. Each layer has its own rate.

## Layer 1 — Direct Members

When Member X's direct downline member A2 completes eligible tasks, Member X will earn **20%** of A2's personal commission as team commission.

## Layer 2 — Second-Level Members

When Member X's second-layer downline member B2 completes eligible tasks, Member X will earn **8%** of B2's personal commission as team commission.

## Layer 3 — Third-Level Members

When Member X's third-layer downline member C2 completes eligible tasks, Member X will earn **6%** of C2's personal commission as team commission.

<Info>
  **Note:** The calculations shown above are based on the Legend Agent Rank. All commission rewards, whether earned by a member, an upline, or a downline, are paid directly by the platform to the eligible recipient. Each commission is calculated independently and is not deducted from any other member's earnings.
</Info>
