title: "Monthly Leadership Bonus"
---

| Valid Layer 1 Members | Valid Layer 2 + Layer 3 Members | Monthly bonus (\$) |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 5 | 7 | \$150 |
| 10 | 15 | \$300 |
| 20 | 60 | \$600 |
| 30 | 100 | \$1,000 |
| 50 | 200 | \$1,500 |
| 60 | 300 | \$5,000 |

## Eligibility and frequency

- Meet the two member-count requirements for the applicable row.
- The second column combines **Layer 2 and Layer 3**; it is not a requirement for each layer separately.
- This bonus can be claimed **once per month**, subject to meeting the required criteria.

## Example

When your team has **5 Valid Layer 1 Members** and **7 Valid Members across Layer 2 and Layer 3**, you are eligible for a **\$150 Monthly Leadership Bonus**.
