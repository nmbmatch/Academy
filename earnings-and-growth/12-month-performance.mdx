title: "12-Month Agent Performance Reward"
---

## Qualification table

| Time period (months) | Team deposits (entire network) | Valid Members (first 3 layers) | Reward (\$) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 12 | \$20,000,000 | 100 | \$250,000 |

## Eligibility and timing

- The qualification period starts on the **date of your first deposit**.
- Total deposits across your **entire network** must reach **at least \$20,000,000** within that period.
- You must have **at least 100 Valid Members within your first 3 layers** within the same period.
- **Both requirements must be met.** Meeting only one does not satisfy the conditions.
- Once all requirements are met, you are eligible to receive **\$250,000**.

## How to check eligibility

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open My Team">
    Open **My Team** in the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check network deposits">
    Go to **Team Growth & Earnings** and check **Total Team Deposits**. Compare the entire-network figure with **\$20,000,000**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Valid Members">
    Go to **Team Member Details**. In the dropdown, select **Total (L1-L3)** and **Valid Members**. Compare the first-three-layer count with **100**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the qualifying period">
    Compare both figures with the 12-month requirements above, measured from your first-deposit date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact your upline">
    **Contact your upline to claim your reward**. Contact your upline as well if you need help checking progress.
  </Step>
</Steps>
