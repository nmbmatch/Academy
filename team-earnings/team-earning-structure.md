# Team Earning Structure

## Layer 1

When Member X’s direct downline member A2 earns **$100** in personal commission, Member X will therefore earn **$20** as team commission.

## Layer 2

When Member X’s second-layer downline member B3 earns **$100** in personal commission, Member X will therefore earn **$8** as team commission.

## Layer 3

When Member X’s third-layer downline member C2 earns **$100** in personal commission, Member X will therefore earn **$6** as team commission.

## Formula

**Your Team Commission = Downline's Commission Earnings × Your Layer Rate**

{% hint style="info" %}
Team Commission is not calculated from the downline's deposit amount or task amount. It is calculated from the commission earnings generated after eligible task completion.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Note:** The calculations shown above are based on the Legend Agent Rank.
{% endhint %}
