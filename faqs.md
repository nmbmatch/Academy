# ⁉️ Faqs

<details>

<summary>Where do rewards come from?</summary>

According to the platform’s business model, rewards are allocated from partner platform service fees to eligible members who complete valid tasks under current platform rules.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do rewards come from user deposits?</summary>

No. Account funds are used for task participation or fulfillment guarantee, not as the reward source.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Does win or loss affect commission?</summary>

No. Commission is based on eligible task completion and system settlement, not match outcomes.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is an eligible task?</summary>

An eligible task is a task displayed by the platform, completed according to its stated requirements, and confirmed as valid by system records.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is the difference between an Active Member and a Valid Member?</summary>

An Active Member has cumulative deposits of at least **$50**. A Valid Member has cumulative deposits of at least **$100**.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is the difference between Personal Commission and Team Commission?</summary>

Personal Commission comes from your own eligible tasks. Team Commission is based on your 1–3 layer members’ eligible revenue, calculated at your rank’s tier rates.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is Team Commission calculated from downline deposits?</summary>

No. Team Commission is calculated from eligible commission earnings generated by members in Layers 1–3, multiplied by your applicable layer rate.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is Agent Rank based only on account balance?</summary>

No. Agent Rank may also require Valid Layer 1 Members and Valid Members in Layers 2–3. All applicable conditions must be met.

</details>
